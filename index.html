About Me

My name is Hanquan Wang. I am currently pursuing M.Sc. in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Washington in the Autonomous Insect Robotics Lab, advised by Sawyer B. Fuller. I earned my B.S. in Mechanical Engineering at UW with a concentration in Mechatronics.

My interests are centered around control systems, robotic manipulation, robot locomotion, and bio-inspired adaptability. I enjoy combining mechanical design, software integration, and precision control to build robotic systems that are both practical and impactful.
